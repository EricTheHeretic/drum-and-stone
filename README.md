# Drum & Stone

Storefront for [drumandstone.com](https://drumandstone.com).

Static site for Drum & Stone LLC, South Fork, Pennsylvania. Products: roasted coffee, bean-to-bar chocolate, beef jerky, dried fruit, and dry noodles.

Checkout is an order note, not a payment form. Do not take money until the Pennsylvania Limited Food Establishment registration is approved.

## Files

- `index.html` — home and goods
- `about.html` — short story
- `styles.css` — camp palette
- `app.js` — product cards and mailto order

## Deploy

Point drumandstone.com at a Vercel project connected to this repo, or any static host. No build step.

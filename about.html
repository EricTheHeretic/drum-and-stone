SOUTH FORK, PENNSYLVANIA

The drum and the stone.

Drum and Stone is a kitchen in South Fork, Cambria County. The name is the roasting drum, and the stone it sits on.

Coffee is roasted from green beans. Chocolate is ground from the bean. Beef is dried as jerky and biltong. Fruit is dried so it keeps. Noodles are an alkaline dough, dried, with no egg.

Nothing is for sale yet. Pennsylvania has to register the kitchen, and the insurance has to be in place, before a bag leaves the house.

The camp kitchen

Made in South Fork.

Drum & Stone is a home food business in South Fork, Cambria County. The name is the roasting drum and the stone under it: coffee, cacao, jerky, dried fruit, and dry noodles, packed for a pack or a pantry.

Coffee is roasted from green beans. Chocolate is bean-to-bar. Jerky and fruit are dried until they keep on a shelf. Noodles are an alkaline dough, dried, no egg.

Sales start after Pennsylvania Department of Agriculture registration. Until then this site takes notes, not payment.

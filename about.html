SOUTH FORK, PENNSYLVANIA

Roasted, dried, and packed here.

Drum and Stone is a home kitchen in South Fork. Coffee is roasted from green beans. Chocolate is ground from the bean. Beef, fruit, and noodles are dried so they keep.

Dried fruit

Dry noodles

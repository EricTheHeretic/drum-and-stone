South Fork, Pennsylvania

The drum, and the stone under it.

A home kitchen in South Fork. Coffee is roasted on the drum. The other foods are dried, ground, or packed on the stone.

CoffeeRoasted from green beans, a small batch at a time.
ChocolateBean to bar, ground fine, set to keep.
BeefDried two ways: jerky, and biltong.
Dried fruitDried down so it keeps in a pack.
Dry noodlesAlkaline dough, dried, no egg.

Nothing is for sale yet. The kitchen has to be registered, and the insurance has to be in place, before a bag leaves South Fork.

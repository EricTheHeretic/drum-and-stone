SOUTH FORK, PENNSYLVANIA

The drum, and the stone under it.

Drum and Stone is a home kitchen in South Fork. Coffee is roasted on the drum. The other foods are dried, ground, or packed on the stone.

Coffee is roasted from green beans, a small batch at a time.

Chocolate is bean to bar, ground fine and set to keep on a shelf.

Beef is dried two ways: jerky, and biltong.

Fruit is dried down so it keeps in a pack.

Noodles are an alkaline dough, dried, with no egg.

Nothing is for sale yet. Pennsylvania has to register the kitchen, and the product liability policy has to be in place, before a bag leaves South Fork.
